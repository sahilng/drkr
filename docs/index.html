Interactive illustration. The actual extension adapts each website.

01 / YOUR CALL

One setting. Or one website.

Choose On, System, or Off. Keep a website’s original look with an override, and let existing dark themes keep their own style.

02 / KEEP THE PICTURE

Photos stay photos.

drkr adapts page colors without inverting photos and videos. Some artwork and complex panels retain their original appearance for readability.

03 / STAYS LOCAL

No browsing business.

No accounts, tracking, analytics, or backend. Preferences stay in Safari’s extension storage on your device.

Open code. A clear starting point.

drkr is available under the MIT license. Inspect the code, test a development build, report a website issue, or help make the web a little easier to read.
