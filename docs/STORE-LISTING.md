## Description

Drkr gives websites a softer, darker appearance in Safari on your Mac. Turn dark mode on, follow your Mac's appearance, or keep a website's original look. Set individual website preferences and let websites with existing dark themes keep their own design.

Photos and videos keep their original colors. Your preferences stay on your device. No accounts, analytics, or tracking.

Drkr needs permission to read and change website appearance. You choose which websites it can access in Safari Settings. Some complex websites, artwork, and embedded applications may keep parts of their original appearance.

Setup: Open Drkr, open Safari Extensions Settings, enable Drkr, allow access to your chosen websites, then reload existing tabs. Click the Drkr toolbar icon for settings.
